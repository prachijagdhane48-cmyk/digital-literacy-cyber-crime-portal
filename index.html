THREAT CENTER

Know the Threats

🎣

Phishing & Social Engineering

Fraudulent messages, websites or callers designed to trick people into sharing confidential information.

⚠️ Watch for:

Urgent requests, suspicious links, misspelled domains and OTP requests.

💳

Financial & UPI Scams

Scams designed to steal money through fraudulent payment requests or fake financial support.

⚠️ Watch for:

Unknown payment requests and callers asking for secret verification codes.

🪪

Identity Theft

Unauthorized use of someone's personal information or identity.

⚠️ Protect yourself:

Keep personal information private and use strong account security.

🦠

Malware & Ransomware

Malicious software that can damage, disrupt or restrict access to data.

⚠️ Stay safe:

Keep software updated and avoid suspicious downloads.
